01 Practice

It watches how you write, not just what.

Write on a genkōyōshi grid with Apple Pencil — or your finger. Ichigo Ichigo reads every stroke — its shape, its direction, the order you drew it — and names what to fix instead of a bare pass or fail.

4-rung ladder traced → blind Live grading per stroke
02 Every character

The whole language, one tap deep.

Over 6,500 kanji and the complete hiragana and katakana. Open any character for stroke order, readings, meaning, what it is built from, and where those pieces appear again — or practise a whole word on a single sheet.

Draw-to-search find it by writing it Components and their echoes
03 Study

A schedule that remembers for you.

Spaced repetition decides what is due and when, and reminds you quietly — even with the app closed. A calm daily queue in place of an endless pile.

Daily snapshots streak & habit Reminders reach a closed app
04 Notebooks

Your own copybooks.

Fill a genkōyōshi notebook from any deck and work through it character by character — left-to-right or the traditional way. A guide shows the next stroke, and the page follows the character you are on.

Fill from a deck any set Auto-focus camera follows you

Bring your own curriculum.

Group characters into decks and levels, or connect your WaniKani account to practise writing exactly what you are learning to read — in WaniKani's own order, with levels, stages, and mnemonics.
